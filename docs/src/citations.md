Please also consider citing the following works that have been foundational to the methods implemented in this package:

* Picard–Lefschetz theory and the Borel plane:
  * Formules de Picard-Lefschetz généralisées et ramification des intégrales, by F. Pham, Bulletin de la Société Mathématique de France 93, 333–367, 1965
  * Hyperasymptotics for multidimensional integrals, exact remainder terms and the global connection problem, by C.J. Howls, Proceedings of the Royal Society A 453, 2271–2294, 1997
* Higher-order Stokes phenomena:
  * [On the higher-order Stokes phenomenon, by C.J. Howls, P.J. Langman and A.B. Olde Daalhuis, 2004](https://doi.org/10.1098/rspa.2004.1299)
  * [Hyperasymptotics for Integrals with Saddles, by M.V. Berry and C.J. Howls, 1991](https://doi.org/10.1098/rspa.1991.0119)
* Homotopy continuation:
  * [A polyhedral method for solving sparse polynomial systems, by B. Huber and B. Sturmfels, 1995](https://doi.org/10.1090/S0025-5718-1995-1297471-4)
  * [The Numerical Solution of Systems of Polynomials Arising in Engineering and Science, by A.J. Sommese and C.W. Wampler, 2005](https://doi.org/10.1142/5763)
* Relevance: the South-East rule:
  * [Which Saddles Contribute? The South-East Rule for Multidimensional Integrals, by I. Aniceto, J. Feldbrugge and C.J. Howls, 2026](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2606.28271)
* Independent evaluation of the Stokes constants:
  * [Asymptotic evaluation of multidimensional oscillatory integrals, by S. Crew and J. Feldbrugge, 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22767), implemented in [IntegralResurgence.jl](https://github.com/jfeldbrugge/IntegralResurgence.jl)
