HomotopyResurgence.followHomotopy — Function
followHomotopy(initial_saddles, Fs, N = 10000, γ = DEFAULT_γ; ρ0 = nothing)

Track all saddle points of γ g into the saddle points of f (Fs = [f, g]). Returns the paths, their Borel singularities t = -i h(x, λ) and, along every path, a continuous branch ρ of √det Hess h (starting from ρ0 when given), which fixes the normalisation 1/ρ of the asymptotic series.

HomotopyResurgence.higherOrderStokes — Method
higherOrderStokes(K, pathsBorel; upto = length(pathsBorel[1]), events = hosEvents(pathsBorel))

Continue the Stokes constants K (K[j, l] = Kjl, normalisations continuous along the paths) through the higher-order Stokes phenomena of the homotopy. When tk crosses the directed segment from tj to tl from its left to its right, Kjl ↦ Kjl + Kjk Kkl, and with the opposite sign when it crosses from right to left (the Borel transform of the series of j passes tk on the other side; the difference is the Stokes jump of j at tk, which then sees tl). The same event updates Klj with the roles of j and l exchanged.

With upto = i, only the events before the grid point i are applied: the Stokes constants at λ = (i - 1)/N, e.g. to draw the adjacency graph midway through the homotopy. The scan for the events dominates the cost for many tracks; pass the events of hosEvents (or the field events returned by stokesHomotopy) to reuse them for several values of upto.

HomotopyResurgence.homotopy — Function
homotopy(p, Fs, N, γ = DEFAULT_γ; escape = 1e6)

Track the saddle point p of γ g (with Fs = [f, g]) along h(x, λ) = λ f(x) + γ (1 - λ) g(x) on the grid λ = 0, 1/N, ..., 1, with adaptive substeps between grid points. A path that leaves the ball |x| < escape, or cannot be continued, goes to infinity: its remaining points are NaN.

HomotopyResurgence.hosEvents — Method
hosEvents(pathsBorel)

Higher-order Stokes events: instants (between the grid points i and i+1) at which the Borel singularity tk crosses the segment between tj and t_l. Returns named tuples (τ, i, j, k, l) with τ the interpolated crossing time and i the last grid point before it, sorted by τ.

The scan visits every triple of tracks, which grows as n³ with the number n of tracks: compute the events once and pass them to higherOrderStokes (keyword events) for every λ.

HomotopyResurgence.initialSaddles — Method
initialSaddles(M)

Saddle points of the factorizable reference system g(x) = Σᵢ cᵢ (xᵢ^mᵢ/mᵢ - xᵢ) (the saddles do not depend on the phases cᵢ), their signed Stokes constants K and the normalisations ρ of the series (up to a factor common to all saddles, which does not affect K). Two saddles are adjacent iff they differ in exactly one coordinate i, with the Stokes constant of the one-dimensional reference system in that coordinate (referenceStokes1D).

HomotopyResurgence.referenceStokes1D — Method
referenceStokes1D(m)

Stokes constants of the reference exponent g(x) = x^m/m - x for the saddles en = e^{2πin/(m-1)}, n = 0, …, m-2, with the continuous normalisation ρn = √(m-1) e^{-iπn/(m-1)} (ρn² = g''(en)): K[j, l] = sign(l - j). Checked against the Gauss-Manin Stokes constants of IntegralResurgence.jl for m = 3, …, 7, and against the large-order relation of the series for m = 3. The constants are unchanged when g is multiplied by a phase c, with ρn → √c ρn.

HomotopyResurgence.referenceSystem — Method
referenceSystem(M; c = goldenPhases(length(M)))

The reference exponent g(x) = Σᵢ cᵢ (xᵢ^mᵢ/mᵢ - xᵢ). Distinct generic phases cᵢ are essential when two mᵢ agree: with cᵢ = 1 different saddles share their critical value and many triples are collinear in the Borel plane, so the reference Stokes constants would be ill defined.

HomotopyResurgence.seriesNormalisation — Method
seriesNormalisation(H, x; normalisation = :morse, atol = 1e-8)

The branch ρ of √det H that normalises the asymptotic series of the saddle point x with Hessian H, T0 = 1/ρ. Stokes constants are only defined once these branches are fixed.

  • :morse (default, as transseries of IntegralResurgence.jl): the principal branch for a complex saddle point and, for a real saddle point with Morse index ν, ρ = i^ν √|det H|, the stationary-phase normalisation.
  • :principal: the principal branch. When det H lies on the negative real axis (up to atol), the branch is fixed at ρ = +i √|det H|; otherwise the sign would depend on the rounding error in the imaginary part of det H.

HomotopyResurgence.stokesHomotopy — Method
stokesHomotopy(f, M; c = goldenPhases(length(M)), γ = DEFAULT_γ, N = 10000, escape = 1e6)

Stokes constants of the saddle points of the exponent f (integral ∫ e^{ik f(x)} dx) by homotopy continuation from the reference system g = Σᵢ cᵢ (xᵢ^mᵢ/mᵢ - xᵢ), with mᵢ - 1 ≥ deg ∂f/∂xᵢ. The saddles of g move along h(x, λ) = λ f(x) + γ(1 - λ) g(x) into the saddles of f or to infinity, and the Stokes constants change at every higher-order Stokes phenomenon on the way.

Returns a named tuple with the saddle points x of f that are reached, their Borel singularities Σ = -i f(x), the Stokes constants K (K[j, l] = K_jl, the constant with which the series of saddle j sees the Borel singularity of saddle l) for the normalisations T0 of the series (see seriesNormalisation), and the tracked paths, pathsBorel and continuous normalisations ρs of all reference saddles, with finite the indices of the paths that reach f. The higher-order Stokes events of the homotopy and the Stokes constants K0 of the reference saddles are returned as well, so that higherOrderStokes(r.K0, r.pathsBorel; upto = i, events = r.events) gives the Stokes constants at any λ without a new scan.

HomotopyResurgence.trackStep — Method

One predictor-corrector step of the saddle point p of h(⋅, λ) from λa to λb. The step is halved until Newton's method converges quickly with a small correction, so that the path cannot jump to a neighbouring saddle point. Returns nothing when the step cannot be completed.
